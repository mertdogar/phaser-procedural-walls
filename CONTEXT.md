# Procedural walls

Tools for authoring and displaying walls in top-down maps.

## Language

**Wall editor**:
The tools for drawing, selecting, and changing walls within a map.

**Wallcraft**:
The standalone wall-authoring application, including its inspector, presets,
map import and export, and playable preview.

**Door**:
A passage barrier belonging to a wall segment, moving with that wall and removed
when the wall is deleted. A closed door blocks movement; an open door permits
passage.

**Hinged door**:
A door that opens by rotating around a hinge at one side of its passage.

**Sliding door**:
A door that opens by moving parallel to its wall.
